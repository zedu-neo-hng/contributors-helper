## Session Overview & Context

Founder address detailing the high-attrition pyramid (top 5% finalists), breaking out of tutorial hell, attention to detail as the primary elimination filter, and teamwork.

---

## Verbatim Transcript & Segments

### `[00:30]` **Mark Essien** (Chief Mentor & Founder)

> "Out of over 13,000 applicants who enter Stage 0, fewer than 5% will reach the finals. This is deliberate. We do not run a traditional school where everyone gets a participation medal. We simulate high-pressure, real-world tech delivery. If you cannot follow basic instructions, format checks, or deadlines, you cannot be trusted with client production code."

### `[04:45]` **Mark Essien** (Chief Mentor & Founder)

> "Stop searching for step-by-step tutorials for every ticket. Real engineering in HNG means reading raw error logs, inspecting Network tabs, and reading the actual codebase. When you are assigned a ticket in Zedu, the codebase is your documentation. Figure it out, ask smart questions in your squad channel, and ship."

### `[09:15]` **Mark Essien** (Chief Mentor & Founder)

> "The number one reason thousands get eliminated in early stages is not lack of coding genius—it is lack of attention to detail. If the ticket says name the branch fix/neo-68-feature-copy, and you name it my-branch, you fail. If the PR description requires a test recording and you leave it blank, you fail. Details are what differentiate junior coders from production engineers."

### `[14:20]` **Seyi Adeleke** (Head of Operations)

> "In stages 0 to 3, you can survive on solo coding. But from stage 4 onward, solo geniuses get eliminated. The product is too large. You must collaborate with your squad lead, communicate in Slack, unblock your teammates, and ensure your PRs do not break someone else's feature. Those who reach the finals get hired because employers know they can function inside real development teams."

---

## Key Action Items & Ground Rules

- **Survival & Attention to Detail:** Fewer than 5% reach finals. Exact adherence to templates, branch naming, and CI gates separates finalists.
- **Read Codebase Directly:** Escape tutorial hell; debug network traffic, console logs, and codebase directly.
- **Teamwork:** Cross-squad communication and unblocking teammates are required.
