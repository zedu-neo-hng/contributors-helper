## Session Overview & Context

Kickoff for Week 2 introducing the Zedu contribution cycle, squad survival mechanics (60-point cutoff, firing inactive members), and the balance between technical development and human taste. Defines the 3 core tasks: Mobile Setup, Desktop Setup, and Ticketing with Taste.

---

## Key Highlights & Directives

1. **Squad Competition & Survival Rules:**
   - Squad survival cutoff: A team must earn at least **60 points** to avoid dissolution/merging.
   - Highest-scoring team wins **₦50,000**.
   - Individual contribution: 1 point per completed task (max 3 points standard).
   - Peer support: 5 nominated helpers per squad receive +1 bonus point.
   - Performance filter: Every squad must identify and fire their 10 least productive/inactive members.
2. **Task 1 — Mobile Setup:**
   - Clone Zedu mobile codebase, build locally, and deploy to physical phone/emulator.
   - The squad agrees on one specific fix (e.g., custom UI icon or label).
   - Every contributor records a video showing the application running on their phone with that squad fix.
3. **Task 2 — Desktop Setup:**
   - Set up the Zedu desktop codebase locally, apply and verify a squad fix, and demonstrate execution.
4. **Task 3 — Ticketing + Human Taste (Web):**
   - Identify a small, high-impact issue on Zedu web.
   - Mark explains the role of human taste in the AI era: AI is an exceptional coder, but humans must supply product judgment, empathy, user understanding, and scope discipline.
   - Non-technical contributors: Focus on marketing, external pages, SEO, and copywriting.
   - Technical contributors: Focus on responsiveness, speed, UX micro-interactions.
   - The approval gate: Tiffany verifies template format -> Mark Essien approves scope and taste (`ready for dev`).
   - Deliverable: Getting the ticket approved (implementation is optional in this initial phase).

## Key Action Items & Ground Rules

- **Taste Over Code:** Carefully evaluate the user experience before proposing a change. Small, thoughtful fixes beat massive, noisy diffs.
- **Two-Step Approval Gate:**
  1. Tiffany validates formatting and checklist completeness.
  2. Mark Essien validates taste, feasibility, and product value.
- **Team Synergy:** Coordinate with squad leads so that everyone successfully runs the mobile and desktop builds with squad-specific modifications.
